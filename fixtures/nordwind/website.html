Moving freight for Northern Europe since 1994

Nordwind Logistics is an independent freight forwarder headquartered in Bergen, with offices in Oslo and Rotterdam. We arrange sea, road and air movements for mid-market exporters and importers, and we handle the customs, documentation and warehousing that go with them.

We are not the largest forwarder on our lanes. We compete on knowing the route, answering the phone, and getting the paperwork right the first time.

Services

Sea freight

Full container load and groupage on the Norway–Continent and Norway–UK trades, working with Sea-Cargo, NCL, Samskip and DFDS. Weekly consolidations from Bergen and Ålesund.

Road freight

Trailer and part-load services to and from the Netherlands, Germany, Denmark and the United Kingdom, including temperature-controlled equipment for the seafood trade.

Customs and documentation

Import and export declarations for Norway, the EU and the United Kingdom. We hold AEO status and can act as your direct or indirect representative.

Warehousing

Bonded and general warehousing in Bergen and Rotterdam, with pick, pack and cross-dock services.

Sectors we know

Seafood and chilled goods, chemicals and dangerous goods, offshore and marine supply, retail and general cargo.

Roughly two thirds of what we move is seafood or chilled product moving south, and industrial and retail goods moving north.

Our lanes

Bergen to Rotterdam. Oslo to Hamburg. Ålesund to Immingham. Bergen to Aarhus. Rotterdam to Immingham.

If your lane is not listed, ask us. We arrange movements outside these routes through partner networks.

Getting a price

Send your enquiry to our quotation desk and a named clerk will handle it from enquiry to booking. Tell us the origin, destination, dimensions and weight, the required date, and whether the goods are hazardous or temperature-controlled.

We aim to respond to standard enquiries within one working day.

About Nordwind

Founded in Bergen in 1994. Approximately 90 staff across three offices. Member of the Norwegian Logistics and Freight Association.

Quality management to ISO 9001. Authorised Economic Operator since 2016.
